MASTER’S THESIS2026 — PRESENT

Long-Range Coulombic Repulsion in Disordered Superconductivity

Prof. Amit Ghosal · IISER Kolkata

I investigate how long-range Coulomb repulsion affects superconductivity in a disordered two-dimensional periodic lattice. In clean superconductors, screening allows the repulsion to be absorbed into a pseudopotential, μ*. With disorder, electron localization weakens screening, making the long-range interaction important.

Within a Bogoliubov–de Gennes mean-field framework, I aim to study how the repulsion modifies the superconducting pairing amplitude, its spatial inhomogeneity, and screening. I am implementing Ewald summation numerically to treat the slowly decaying 1/r interaction under periodic boundary conditions, splitting it into real-space and reciprocal-space sums.

PROJECT2025 — PRESENT

Sub-Planckian Structures & Quantum Geometry of Nonclassical States

Prof. Sourin Das · IISER Kolkata

Studied coherent-state formalisms (Weyl–Heisenberg, SU(2), SU(1,1)) and Wigner-function descriptions of cat and compass states. Explored sub-Planckian interference features, displacement sensitivity, N-component cat states, and Wigner and Husimi-Q functions. Compared entropy-based measures with quantum Fisher information and fidelity, derived the relation between quantum metric and quantum Fisher information, and am studying geometric phase and geodesic evolution on the quantum-state manifold.

Wigner functionsQuantum metrologyQuantum geometry
PROJECT2025 — 2026

Periodically Driven Quantum Spin Liquid

Prof. Vishwanath Shukla · IIT Kharagpur

Studied the Kitaev honeycomb model and its fractionalization into Majorana fermions and gauge sectors. Used Jordan–Wigner and Majorana fermionization approaches, extended the Majorana formulation to Floquet unitary operators for periodic drives, and analytically derived energy and quasienergy spectra under two-dimensional periodic boundary sectors. Also investigated prethermalization and thermalization using high-frequency expansions.

EXPERIMENTAL RESEARCH · PPMS2025

Quantum Transport in Disordered Tungsten Thin Films

Dr. Partha Mitra · IISER Kolkata

Measured the resistivity of three approximately 20 nm tungsten films (α, β, and mixed phase) as a function of temperature—down to 0.34 K using a He-3 probe—and magnetic field in a Physical Property Measurement System (PPMS), using the van der Pauw method.

Analyzed low-temperature conductivity in terms of weak localization and variable-range hopping. Interpreted the magnetoresistance as negative at low fields (suppression of weak localization), positive at intermediate fields (two-band model), and saturating at high fields, with a crossover to classical behavior near 3 K.

INTERNSHIPSUMMER 2025

Topological Phases in 1D Quantum Lattice Models

Prof. Vishwanath Shukla · IIT Kharagpur

Studied the Su–Schrieffer–Heeger chain: Bloch Hamiltonians, band structure, density of states, and discrete symmetries. Diagonalized finite open chains to track zero-energy edge states and their finite-size splitting; computed winding number, Zak phase, and polarization to establish bulk–edge correspondence.

INTERNSHIPSUMMER 2024

Thermal Phenomena & Brownian Motion

Prof. Arindam Kundagrami · IISER Kolkata

Studied momentum, heat, and particle transport using continuity equations, Fick’s laws, and kinetic theory. Derived transport coefficients for a dilute gas and examined Brownian motion through Einstein’s diffusion approach and the Langevin equation, connected by the Stokes–Einstein relation.
